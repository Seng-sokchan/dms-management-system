Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-20

This Privacy Policy explains how DMS (Distribution Management System) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our mobile app and related services at dmscambodia.com.

Who we are

DMS is operated for distribution ordering, depot operations, and delivery tracking in Cambodia. Contact: admin@dmscambodia.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, username, email (if provided), role, and authentication credentials.
  • Order and catalog activity: items ordered, quantities, depot/customer links, and delivery status history.
  • Precise location: while delivery or tracking features are in use, so dispatch and customers can follow active deliveries.
  • Payment-related data needed to process ABA PayWay checkouts (transaction references and payment status). We do not store full card numbers in the DMS app.
  • Technical data needed to run the service securely (for example session tokens and basic device/app diagnostics).

How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • To create, prepare, and fulfill orders between customers, depots, and delivery agents.
  • To share live delivery location with authorized dispatch, depot, and customer views while a delivery is active.
  • To start and confirm online payments through our payment provider.
  • To provide support and improve reliability of the service.

Sharing

We share data only as needed to operate DMS: with authorized staff roles inside your organization, and with service providers such as ABA PayWay for payments and map providers for map display. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

We keep account and order records as long as needed for operations, accounting, and legal obligations. Location updates used for live tracking are retained according to operational needs and then removed or archived.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the Profile screen in the DMS mobile app (Delete account). This deactivates your login so you can no longer sign in. You may also email admin@dmscambodia.com to request deletion. Some order and payment records may be retained where required for business or legal reasons.

Security

We use HTTPS and access controls to protect data in transit and limit who can see operational information. No method of transmission is 100% secure; please keep your password confidential.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact admin@dmscambodia.com.
